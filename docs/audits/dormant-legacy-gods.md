# Dormant and legacy catalog gods — 協紀 day rules (P3a, audit only)

Status: **AUDIT ONLY — no presence, score, status or verdict change.** Date 2026-10-10. Branch `audit/dormant-gods`.
Related: [truc-than-context-audit.md](truc-than-context-audit.md) §2, [xieji-monthly-sha-presence.md](xieji-monthly-sha-presence.md), `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` §5 P3.

## Question

Of the 44 scoring gods, 21 never fire (no exact name in the day star table, no day rule) and 14 fire from legacy `stars.data.ts` tables evaluated on the **lunar** month, while 協紀 defines them by **Month Jian** (月建). For each of these 35 gods: what day rule does 協紀 adopt, and how far are the legacy tables from it?

## Method

- Source: 欽定協紀辨方書 (四庫全書本), Wikisource raw wikitext 卷三–卷十 (SHA-1 prefixes in `shared/almanac-data/xieji-god-definitions.json`; 卷三 `ce833ba7e0d3`, 卷四 `da680c13601d`, 卷五 `1d4000974e32`, 卷六 `a9ab67896fc9`, 卷十 `355dc6c8fe2d` — the same files earlier audits used).
- An agent extracted the adopted rule per god (preferring the book's own 按 over quoted almanacs), with exact quotes and character offsets. All 100 quotes were re-verified by script against the raw files; eleven gods (普護, 福生, 聖心, 益後, 續世, 五富, 歸忌, 往亡, 天喜, 天倉, 時德) were re-read by hand.
- Cross-checks in `shared/lunar/xiejiGodDefinitions.test.ts`: the tables equal the locked modules (`xiejiMonthlySha`: 月厭/月刑/月煞/災煞; `xiejiVirtues`: 天赦/天願/月恩/四相) and the frozen Group B v3.1 tables (災煞, 月煞) on all 12 × 60 cells; 天喜 = 成日 and 三合 = 定日 + 成日 as 協紀 states. The agent also ran the 卷十 「X月值Y日」 statements as 建除 assertions: all usable statements agree (one 卷十 note, 天願不得與遊禍併, fails in months 4 and 10 for every reading of 天願; the 天願 table is confirmed elsewhere, so the note is the inconsistent part).
- Legacy comparison: for each legacy/alias day-table entry, compare its `byMonth` row m with the 協紀 row for month m over all 60 pillars, then count 2025–2027 days by presence basis (legacy: lunar month; 協紀: Month Jian).

## Findings

1. **All 35 gods have a text-defined rule.** None depends on an image-only table. Sources: mostly 卷四–卷六 義例; 四忌 from 神煞起例; 天喜 from 卷四 (按 adopts 成日 and rejects 春戌 夏丑 秋辰 冬未).
2. **Every legacy table is the 協紀 table.** All 14 legacy lunar-month gods and all 5 day-table names whose identity was unproven equal the 協紀 rule in **12/12 months**. The tables are right. Only the month basis is wrong: about **20–30% of their days** (e.g. 三合 45 legacy-only + 44 協紀-only days of 181) fall on the wrong side of a 節 boundary.
3. **Five aliases are now proven by table identity:** Phổ Hộ (Hội Hộ) = 普護, Phi Ma Sát (Tai Sát) = 災煞, Nguyệt Hư (Nguyệt Sát) = 月煞, Nguyệt Yếm Đại Hoạ = 月厭, Vãng Vong (Thổ Kỵ) = 往亡. Bridging them by name was blocked only because identity was unproven (`truc-than-context-audit.md` §2); a bridge should still use the Month Jian rule, not the lunar table.
4. **16 dormant gods have no day-table entry at all** but a clean definition: 時德, 五合, 不將, 天倉, 月害, 厭對, 大時, 四廢, 四忌, 四窮, 五墓, 五離, 八專, 歸忌 (plus 天願/四相, already coded in `xiejiVirtues`).
5. **Points needing a decision before any scoring change:**
   - 往亡 vs 氣往亡: 卷十 uses both; the catalog `vang_vong` (往亡) is the branch rule. 氣往亡 (counted from each 節: 立春後7日 … 小寒後30日) is a separate god, not in the catalog.
   - 不將: 按 accepts the 歴例 list but calls 六月戊午 逐陣 (unusable); kept in the table, flagged.
   - 五富: 按 adopts 順行四孟 but keeps 「或曰當逆行四孟」 as plausible (medium confidence).
   - 三合: 歴例 text reads 「十二月在丑巳」 (corrupt); normalized to 巳酉 by 考原 and 卷十 「三合即成定日」.
   - Readings: 月恩 四月 巳 → 己, 四相 夏 戊巳 → 戊己, 不將 八月 癸己 → 癸巳, 卷三 寅刑己 → 寅刑巳.
